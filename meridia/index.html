Meridia Daily Alignment

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Meridia is private by design. Your birth profile and journal are stored locally, without a Meridia account or developer-operated journal server. Optional weather requests send approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo. The app contains no advertising or third-party tracking.

What Meridia stores, and where

To create your readings, Meridia keeps the following on your device only, inside the app's own storage:

  • Your name and birth details (date, time, and place) to calculate your chart and numerology.
  • Your journal entries, moods, intentions, dream notes, and synchronicities.
  • Your daily readings, streaks, and app settings.

Meridia does not automatically send this journal or profile information to the developer. You may choose to export journal text through the iOS share sheet; the destination you choose will receive the exported content. Your device backup settings may also include app data. Meridia does not provide automatic journal synchronization.

Location

If you use the weather attunement feature, Meridia asks for your location once to read your local conditions. Your approximate coordinates are sent to the free weather service Open-Meteo solely to retrieve current weather, and are not stored by Meridia. If you decline location access, the rest of the app works normally.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning reminder, Meridia schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. This is handled entirely by iOS on your phone. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

None. Meridia includes no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking technologies. We do not build a profile of you and we do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because there is nothing to track.

Third-party services

The only external service Meridia contacts is Open-Meteo, and only when you use weather. The request includes coordinates rounded to two decimal places. Like other internet services, Open-Meteo also receives network information such as your IP address. Meridia uses a temporary network session without a persistent response cache. Open-Meteo states that it may retain technical server logs, including IP addresses and geographical coordinates, for up to 90 days for troubleshooting, maintenance and abuse prevention. These records are not used by Meridia for advertising or tracking. See Open-Meteo’s privacy information for its retention and handling of requests.

Deleting your data

Changing your birth profile preserves your journal and history. Deleting the app from your device removes its local app data. Copies you exported, or copies held in device backups, are managed separately through those destinations and your Apple backup settings.

Children

Meridia is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

For reflection, not advice

Meridia's astrology, tarot, numerology, and frequency content is offered for personal reflection and entertainment. It is not medical, psychological, financial, or professional advice, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
