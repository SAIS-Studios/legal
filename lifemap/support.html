Help & support

Keep the voice. Tell the story.

Start a story

Choose a question, then record your answer or select “Type it instead.” Review your words and save the story. The free plan saves two stories; LifeMap Lifetime unlocks additional story capacity with a one-time purchase.

Return to an unfinished draft

Use the “Continue your draft” card on the home screen. “Save draft and close” keeps unfinished work on this device. If a recording was interrupted, check it before relying on it as your only copy.

Why is transcription unavailable?

Your device, selected language and speech permissions must support on-device recognition. Save the original recording and add a transcript later if recognition is unavailable. Manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Export your stories

Open “Your book” to create a written PDF. Open an individual story to export its recording separately. Save copies before deleting the app or moving to another device. Physical printing is not included.

Restore your purchase

Use “Restore purchases” on the upgrade screen while signed into the Apple Account used for purchase. The LifeMap Lifetime launch offer is a one-time purchase, with no recurring charge. The price is shown in the app before purchase.

Get help

Contact Eric Stewart through his public contact page. Include your device model, iOS version, LifeMap version and a description of the issue. Please avoid sending personal stories or recordings unless you choose to share them.
