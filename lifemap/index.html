Privacy policy

LifeMap by Eric Stewart · Updated September 8, 2026

LifeMap helps you record and write personal life stories. The current app stores your story collection and unfinished drafts on your device. You do not need a LifeMap account.

Stories and recordings

The names, stories, transcripts and recordings you add are stored in the app’s local files and database. LifeMap does not send these stories to a developer-operated server. Automatic LifeMap cloud synchronization and remote family collaboration are not enabled.

Microphone and transcription

Microphone access is requested when you choose to record. Speech recognition permission is requested for transcription. LifeMap requires on-device speech recognition; when your device or language does not support it, you can keep the recording and type a transcript. You can decline these permissions and write stories instead.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple processes payments and provides purchase-entitlement information to the app. LifeMap does not receive your payment-card details. The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or a third-party analytics service. Apple may process App Store transactions and system diagnostics under your Apple settings and its privacy policy.

Exports, backups and deletion

You control exports through the iOS share sheet. A destination you choose, such as a cloud drive, printer or messaging app, handles that copy under its own policies. PDFs contain written stories; audio is exported separately.

Draft recovery is local and does not replace a backup. Your device’s system backup settings may separately govern backups of app data. Export copies before changing devices or deleting the app. Settings → Delete everything removes LifeMap’s saved collection and drafts from this device. Previously exported or backed-up copies must be removed separately from their destinations.

Your choices

Use iOS Settings to manage microphone and speech permissions. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting LifeMap does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Contact

For privacy questions, use Eric Stewart’s public contact page. You do not need to send private recordings to request help.
